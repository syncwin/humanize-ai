# Before and After: Transformation Examples

**Source:** house reference (SyncWin). Examples 11 to 17 illustrate patterns documented in [Wikipedia:Signs of AI writing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing) (revision 1377935935) and Graphite's AI Tells research (Source 5). All names, companies, and figures in the examples are invented for illustration.

**Last verified:** 2026-10-05

Concrete pairs: an AI-style input and a rewrite. Use this file when unsure how to rewrite a pattern: find the closest match and apply the same logic. Every rewrite keeps only facts the "before" already contained. Where a sentence needs a fact the source doesn't give, the rewrite says less or asks; it never invents one.

---

## 1. Dead Openers

**Before:**
"In today's fast-paced digital landscape, content creators must understand the pivotal role that consistency plays in building a loyal audience."

**After:**
"Most content creators underestimate consistency. They watch how often they post instead."

---

**Before:**
"It is important to note that building trust with your audience takes time and cannot be rushed."

**After:**
"Building trust takes time, and you can't rush it."

---

## 2. Negative Parallelism

**Before:**
"It's not about posting more. It's about posting smarter. It's not about chasing trends. It's about creating value."

**After:**
"Posting smarter beats posting more. Creating value outlasts chasing trends."

---

**Before:**
"It was never about the algorithm. It was always about the relationship."

**After:**
"Your relationship with the audience matters more than the algorithm."

---

**Before:**
"The real issue is not the tools you use. It is how you think."

**After:**
"How you think determines what the tools produce."

---

## 3. Vocabulary Replacement

**Before:**
"This cutting-edge methodology will revolutionize the way you approach content creation, empowering you to harness unparalleled synergies across your entire digital ecosystem."

**After:**
"This approach changes how you create content and connects the separate parts of your workflow."

---

**Before:**
"Meticulously crafted to seamlessly integrate with your existing robust infrastructure, this solution offers a holistic framework that fosters growth."

**After:**
"It is built to work with what you already have. The framework helps you grow without rebuilding everything."

---

## 4. Inflated Verb Phrases

**Before:**
"This platform serves as a comprehensive resource that plays a pivotal role in empowering creators to unlock their full potential."

**After:**
"This platform gives creators the tools to do their best work."

---

**Before:**
"The report boasts a detailed analysis that is designed to help stakeholders navigate the complexities of modern market dynamics."

**After:**
"The report breaks down current market conditions clearly enough for stakeholders to act on."

---

## 5. AI Sentence Structure Templates

**Before (Triple Attribute Sequence):**
"Focused. Aligned. Measurable. These are the three pillars of every great strategy."

**After:**
"A good strategy is focused, tied to a measurable outcome, and shared by the people executing it."

---

**Before (Fragmented Q&A Loop):**
"And the result? Exponential growth. And the secret? Consistency. And the cost? Almost nothing."

**After:**
"Consistent effort over time produced significant growth, and it cost almost nothing to start."

---

**Before (Symmetric Dual-Action):**
"This tool not only saves you time, but it also dramatically improves the quality of your output."

**After:**
"This tool saves time and produces better output."

---

**Before (Conditional Inclusivity Frame):**
"Whether you're a solopreneur just starting out or a seasoned executive leading a team of fifty, the principles in this guide apply to you."

**After:**
"The principles here apply whether you are running things alone or managing a large team."

---

## 6. Tone Patterns: Fake Authority and Uniform Enthusiasm

**Before:**
"The paradigm-shifting intersection of AI and human creativity represents a groundbreaking testament to our collective potential. This transformative moment in history will undoubtedly redefine the very fabric of how we work, create, and innovate."

**After:**
"AI is changing how people do creative work."

The "before" makes one claim wrapped in hype; the "after" keeps the claim and drops the hype rather than adding new opinions.

---

**Before:**
"Every single piece of content you publish is a monumental opportunity to connect with your audience in a meaningful, impactful, and transformative way."

**After:**
"Each piece you publish is a chance to connect with your audience."

---

## 7. Puffery and Participle Padding

**Before:**
"This marked a pivotal turning point in the company's journey, setting the stage for a new era of growth, highlighting its commitment to innovation and paving the way for future success."

**After:**
"This changed the company's direction."

If the source says what followed (revenue, headcount, a launch), add that fact. Don't supply one.

---

## 8. Weak Hedging Qualifiers

**Before:**
"It is important to note that results may vary, and it could potentially be suggested that individual outcomes are impacted by a variety of contextual factors."

**After:**
"Results vary. What works in one context may not work in another."

---

## 9. Rhythm: Uniform Sentence Length

**Before (all medium-length, all similar structure):**
"Great content starts with a clear idea. You should always know your audience well. Your message needs to be specific and direct. Every piece should have a single goal."

**After (varied length and rhythm):**
"Great content starts with one clear idea. Not a theme: a specific thing you are trying to say. Know your audience. Know what you want them to do when they finish reading."

---

## 10. Endings

**Before:**
"In conclusion, we have explored the key factors that contribute to successful content strategy, and it is clear that a holistic, data-driven approach will empower creators to elevate their brand and achieve unparalleled growth in today's dynamic landscape."

**After:**
"Pick one of these and try it this week. See what comes back. That feedback tells you more than any framework."

---

## 11. Vague association (Wikipedia: "vague expression of connection")

*Source available: Priya Shah was CTO of Ledgerly from 2019 to 2022.*

**Before:**
"Shah has been closely associated with Ledgerly's technology leadership and is connected to the wider fintech ecosystem."

**After:**
"Shah was Ledgerly's CTO from 2019 to 2022."

---

## 12. Notability claims instead of content (Wikipedia: "canned emphasis on notability")

*Source available: a 2025 review in a city newspaper praised the bakery's sourdough.*

**Before:**
"The bakery has been featured in multiple regional media outlets and maintains an active social media presence, underscoring its growing recognition."

**After:**
"A 2025 review in the city's daily paper singled out its sourdough."

---

## 13. Importance flagging, tradeoff avoidance, helpfulness phrasing (Graphite)

**Before:**
"Why caching matters: every extra second of load time costs sales. The new cache, which refreshes every five minutes, makes it easier to serve pages quickly without sacrificing freshness and can help you avoid costly slowdowns."

**After:**
"Every extra second of load time costs sales. The new cache serves pages from memory and refreshes them every five minutes, so a page can be up to five minutes out of date."

The "after" states the tradeoff the "before" hid.

---

## 14. Pseudo-specific numbers (Graphite: concrete-sounding numbers and timeframes)

*No source gives these figures.*

**Before:**
"In about twenty minutes, a handful of template tweaks can cut your bounce rate within two or three weeks."

**After:**
"A few template changes can lower your bounce rate. How much depends on where visitors leave now, so check the exit-pages report first."

---

## 15. Definition by contrast (Wikipedia: negative parallelism; Graphite: "less like a _ and more like")

**Before:**
"A content brief is less like a checklist and more like a map, guiding writers rather than simply constraining them."

**After:**
"A content brief tells the writer who the piece is for and what that reader should do after reading it."

---

## 16. Canned change notes (Wikipedia: edit summaries)

**Before:**
"Refined the article for clarity and flow, enhanced readability, and ensured a neutral tone while preserving the original meaning."

**After:**
"Cut the 'Despite these challenges' closing paragraph, changed 'serves as' to 'is' in four places, and left every price and date as it was."

---

## 17. Leaked citation markup (Wikipedia: internal formatting bugs)

**Before:**
"The program launched in 2024 and now serves 1,200 schools. citeturn0search1"

**After:**
"The program launched in 2024 and now serves 1,200 schools."

Plus a note to the author: the `turn0search` marker means a chatbot supplied this claim, so find and cite the real source before publishing.
