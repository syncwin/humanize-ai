## Graphite: "AI Tells" research (Source 5)

## StoryScope (Source 3)

`knowledge/source-3-research-paper/narrative-structure-tells.md` paraphrases findings from Jenna Russell, Rishanth Rajendhran, Chau Minh Pham, Mohit Iyyer, and John Wieting, "StoryScope: Investigating idiosyncrasies in AI fiction," COLM 2026 ([arXiv:2604.03136](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.03136)). Statistics are summarized for practical use, not copied from the paper.

## Detector methodology (Source 4)

`knowledge/source-4-detector-methodology/statistical-detector-signals.md` describes publicly documented concepts behind AI-text detectors (perplexity, burstiness, watermarking, provenance), not any vendor's proprietary method, and cites the studies it relies on in the file. Product names (GPTZero, Pangram, Originality.ai, Copyleaks, Turnitin, ZeroGPT, SynthID) belong to their owners and are mentioned for identification only.
